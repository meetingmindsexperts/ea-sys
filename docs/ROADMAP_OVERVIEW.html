Decisions we need

Each one unblocks work that is ready or nearly ready. Tap one for the detail.

Account sign-up: inside EA-SYS, or a separate app?MedhatBuilt and paused for approval. About a day to finish if we keep it in EA-SYS.

Most urgent

Keeping it in EA-SYS reuses the security we already have (confirmation emails, login limits). A separate app on our API gives more freedom in design, but it is a second system to build and look after.

Add an "Interested" button to events?MedhatSigned-in people save events and opt in to reminders. Needs account sign-up first.

About two days of work after sign-up. Reminders need their own opt-in. The count of interested people stays internal.

List partner events that register elsewhere?MedhatAn event page with a button to the organiser's own registration.

Useful for partner events now, and the first step toward listing-only customers such as DHA or EHS.

Show every new webinar in the catalogue by default?Events teamToday it is a tick-box per event.

Webinars do not appear today because nobody has ticked the box. Defaulting to on would list them automatically.

Which e-invoicing provider?FinanceMust connect to QuickBooks. Due by 31 March 2027.

A first call with Averyx's tax team is requested for 9 November. They advise on tax; we handle the systems side.

Share past event budgets?FinanceNeeded before EA-SYS can compare events and draft budgets.

A simple sheet per past event (category and amount) is enough. The suggestions never set an amount on their own; a person approves every budget.

Key dates

Only the e-invoicing dates are fixed by law. The rest move once the decisions above are made.
