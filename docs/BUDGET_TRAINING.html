Budget training pack

Everything for one session that gets the office familiar with Budgets: a live demo, then everyone practises their own part on a training event. The session agenda, the setup to do beforehand, the clean-up afterwards, and a one-page sheet for each role.

  • 1Agenda
  • 2Setup, the day before
  • 3Clean-up, the same day
  • 4Role sheets

Practice happens on the live system, fenced off

  • Everything uses a draft event called Budget Training (code TRAIN2026), which is never public.
  • Emails are real: approvers get real approval emails. That is part of the practice.
  • Numbers are real. The session will use the first request and order numbers of the year, starting at PR-2026-0001 and PO-2026-0001. They end up cancelled with the reason "Training session, void". Tell the accountant which numbers these were. The numbers start again at 0001 each January.
  • Nothing reaches the accounts: EA-SYS reads from QuickBooks and never writes to it.
  • Clean up the same day. A request left waiting gets a reminder after 24 hours and is passed on after 48, so a forgotten practice request turns into real emails for real people.

2. Setup, the day before

Done by a super admin, in the app. About 30 minutes.

A. Decide who plays which part

PartWhoRole to give (Settings › Users, wallet icon)
RequesterMost of the officeRequester
Budget authorProject managersPO Author
ApproverTwo or three managersPO Approver, plus an amount in Approve up to (AED), e.g. 2,000 for one and 5,000 for another
Final approverThe admin who already has unlimited approvalNothing to change
FinanceAccountsFinance Settle

Two combinations the system refuses

  • The final approver cannot also be a requester (their own request would have nobody above them).
  • Finance sign-off cannot sit with approving (that puts the approval and the check in one pair of hands).

A role that approves does nothing until an amount is set on the person. Changes reach people within five minutes; ask them to sign out and in again if a menu is missing.

B. Create the training event

  1. Events › New event: name Budget Training, any dates, leave it as Draft.
  2. Open the event's Settings and set Event Code to TRAIN2026. A budget needs a code.

C. Add two practice suppliers

  1. Budgets › Suppliers › Propose a supplier: TRAINING Venue Co and TRAINING Print Co, currency AED, country UAE.
  2. For the contact email, use the trainer's own mailbox, never a real supplier's, so a "Send to supplier" during practice lands with you.
  3. Approve both (the Finance Settle holder, a super admin, or the final approver can). Only an approved supplier can carry an order.

D. Create and approve the training budget

  1. Budgets › New budget: event Budget Training, currency AED, contingency 10%, expected attendance 50.
  2. Fill three lines with the pencil icon: 510400 Venue AED 4,000; 500300 Design, Marketing & Production AED 2,000; 510200 Speakers & Faculty AED 2,000.
  3. Click Mark the remaining N not applicable, then Submit for approval.
  4. The final approver approves it under Budgets › Approvals. The budget is now Active and ready for requests.

Tip: keep a second draft budget unfinished if you want to show filling a budget during the demo.

E. Check

  • Sign in as one requester (or ask one) and confirm Budgets shows in the sidebar.
  • Have a sample quote ready to attach: any PDF, PNG or JPEG under 10 MB.

3. Clean-up, the same day

Right after the session. About 15 minutes, by the trainer with an admin or Finance Settle account.

  1. Orders: on each training request that has an order, open the order section. If it was received, Undo receipt first (the person who marked it, or an admin). Then Cancel order with the reason Training session, void. The request goes back to its approver.
  2. Requests: cancel every training request (draft, waiting or approved) with the same reason. None may stay waiting overnight.
  3. Suppliers: deactivate TRAINING Venue Co and TRAINING Print Co.
  4. Budget: leave it. It can stay as the practice budget for new joiners, or be closed out.
  5. Approval amounts: remove any amount you set only for the session (Settings › Users).
  6. Accountant: send the range of numbers used, e.g. PR-2026-0001 to PR-2026-0020 and PO-2026-0001 to PO-2026-0015, all void.
  7. Check next morning: Budgets › Approvals shows nothing from TRAIN2026 waiting.

4. Role sheets

One page each; they print on separate pages. The same guidance is in the user guide, chapter 22, so the help chat can answer from it.

Requester: asking for money

You need something bought for an event.

  1. Budgets › Requests › New request.
  2. Pick the event's budget and the line the cost belongs to (Venue, Printing, ...).
  3. Give a title, the amount before VAT, and the VAT as a rate (type 5 for 5%).
  4. Choose the supplier. Not on the list? Propose a vendor; Finance approves it.
  5. Say how it was sourced (single quote, competitive quotes), when it is needed, and why.
  6. Save as draft, attach at least one quote, then Submit.

Watch the panel on the right: it shows what the line has left, whether you are within budget, and who will decide.

Over budget? You can still submit with a justification. It goes to the final approver.

After approval the purchase order appears on your request with its PO number and a PDF. When the goods arrive, click Mark received.

You can withdraw a request while it waits, and cancel it with a reason. You never approve your own request.

Approver: deciding

You have been given an approval amount.

  1. You get an email when something waits for you. The email only notifies: decide in the app.
  2. Budgets › Approvals › Waiting on me.
  3. Open the item: the amount, the budget line, what the line has left, the supplier and the quotes.
  4. Approve (note optional) or Reject (note required: the requester needs to know what to change).

Approving a request issues the purchase order at once and commits the amount on the budget line.

Timing: a reminder after 24 hours; after 48 hours your stand-in (or the next approver) can decide too; after 96 hours it moves up. Decide the same day.

You only see requests within your amount. Larger ones and over-budget exceptions go higher.

Finance: suppliers, orders, sign-off

You hold the Finance Settle role.

  1. Suppliers: Budgets › Suppliers, the Proposed queue. Check the tax number, terms and currency, then approve or reject with a note. Bank details are yours to enter.
  2. Orders: open a request to see its order: number, lines, PDF. Send to supplier emails the PDF to the supplier's contact.
  3. Receiving: a full receipt of AED 50,000 or more needs a second person to Confirm receipt, never the one who marked it.
  4. Cancelling: only while nothing is received. Cancel order with a reason; the amount is released and the request goes back to its approver. Tell the supplier yourself.
  5. Sign off: after the author closes a budget, review the summary and click Sign off.

"Actual" shows 0.00 until EA-SYS reads paid figures from the accounts. Read "Committed" for what has been ordered.

Budget author: the event's plan

You hold the PO Author role, usually a project manager.

  1. The event needs an Event Code (event Settings) first.
  2. Budgets › New budget: event, currency, contingency %, expected attendance.
  3. Fill a line per expected cost with the pencil: description, quantity, unit cost, VAT rate. Planned is before VAT.
  4. Every category must have a line or be marked not applicable (click its chip).
  5. Submit for approval. Once approved the budget is Active and people can raise requests.

Plans change: Move an amount between lines (up to 10% of a line is yours; more asks for approval). A big change is a New version.

After the event: Close out, write a note for any line out by more than 10% or AED 5,000, then close. Finance signs off.
