1

Raise

A request starts as a draft against one line of an active budget. The form reads the line as you type, so the requester sees the answer before anyone else does.

2

Submit

Submitting runs the budget check once more on the stored figures and opens an approval request. Nothing is committed yet: a submitted request is the ask, not the order.

3

Approve

Approvals are taken on the Approvals page, never from email. Each approver sees only what routed to them.

4

Order

An approved request becomes a purchase order by itself. Nobody raises the order by hand in the normal course.

5

Receive

Receiving records that the goods or services arrived. Large orders need a second pair of eyes.

6

Cancel and change

An order is never edited. A change is a cancel and a fresh approval, so the order a supplier holds never disagrees with ours and nobody can undo a cancel alone.

7

When an approver is slow

A request never sits unnoticed. EA-SYS emails, reminds, adds a delegate and escalates on a fixed clock, and the requester is emailed the decision.

8

Find

Every request is on one list, and every approver has a record of their own decisions.

Words used here

Spend request
The ask: what someone wants to buy, against which line, for how much. It carries a PR number.
Purchase order
The order an approved request becomes, issued on approval once its supplier is approved. It carries a PO number and is never edited.
Line
One row of a budget: planned, committed, actual, and what is left.
Committed
What the line's open purchase orders hold. Issuing an order raises it; cancelling one releases it.
Exception
A request that would take its line below zero, or any request past the line on a frozen budget. It needs a reason and only the final approver decides it.
Ceiling
The largest amount in AED an approver may decide. A request routes to the lowest ceiling that covers it.
Settle holder
The person who approves suppliers, confirms large receipts and cancels orders. They never approve requests.
Delegate
The person who can decide beside an approver once a request has waited 48 hours.
Awaiting supplier
Approved money whose vendor is not yet an approved supplier; the order is issued the moment the supplier is approved.
