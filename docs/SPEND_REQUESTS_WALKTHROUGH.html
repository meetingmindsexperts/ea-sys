Spend Requests Walkthrough

How a spend request moves from a draft on one budget line to an approved amount, shown screen by screen from the module as built.

1

Raise

A request starts as a draft against one line of an active budget. The form reads the line as you type, so the requester sees the answer before anyone else does.

2

Submit

Submitting runs the budget check once more on the stored figures and opens an approval request. Nothing is committed yet: a submitted request is the ask, not the order.

3

Approve

Approvals are taken on the Approvals page, never from email. Each approver sees only what routed to them.

4

Change

An approved amount can still change. The direction decides what happens.

5

Find

Every request is on one list, and every approver has a record of their own decisions.

What comes next

  • An approved request with an approved supplier will turn into a commitment, a purchase order with its own PO number, in the next slice. Until then approval is the end of the road on this page and the figure is recorded as agreed money against the line.
  • Receiving against the order, the second-person rule on receipt, quote files as attachments, and the MCP tools for listing and raising requests follow in the same slice.

Words used here

Spend request
The ask: what someone wants to buy, against which line, for how much. It carries a PR number.
Purchase order
The commitment produced when an approved request is placed with an approved supplier. It carries a PO number. Not yet built.
Line
One row of a budget: planned, committed, actual, and what is left.
Exception
A request that would take its line below zero, or any request past the line on a frozen budget. It needs a reason and only the final approver decides it.
Ceiling
The largest amount in AED an approver may decide. A request routes to the lowest ceiling that covers it.
Awaiting supplier
Approved money whose vendor is not yet an approved supplier on the master.
