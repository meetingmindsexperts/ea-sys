Webinar Troubleshooting

What can go wrong with a webinar, how to recognise it, and how to fix it yourself. Written for the webinar team running webinars in EA-SYS, Nov 24, 2026, from the screens and messages as they are today. Organised by what you or an attendee sees. Words in quotation marks are exactly what appears on screen. A Developer mark means the fix needs someone with access to the server.

1. Five facts that explain most problems

  1. The room opens only when you open it. Attendees wait in the waiting room until someone presses "Open the room / Go live" in the Webinar Console. Starting Zoom as host does not let anyone in, and the room never opens by itself at the start time.
  2. Who may watch: someone with a registration that is Confirmed (or Checked in) and paid, complimentary, or with nothing to pay. Pending approval, waiting list and unpaid registrations are kept out. Staff of the organisation can always get in, which is why a test as staff can look fine when attendees cannot.
  3. Attendees cannot get in more than 15 minutes before the scheduled start. Before that they see when join opens.
  4. Closing the room after the start ends the webinar. Attendees get the thank-you screen and the survey. It is not a pause. To continue, use "Re-open the room".
  5. A Draft event behaves differently. Its room counts as open the whole time, and only staff can see it. Test on a published event, or remember that Draft is not what attendees will get.

3. Before the day: a checklist

  • The event is Published (event Settings, General, "Status").
  • A registration rate is switched on and ticked for the main Register link (Registration Types page).
  • The Webinar Console shows a Zoom webinar attached (no "Zoom webinar not provisioned" banner), or, for a partner's webinar, the key is issued and handed over.
  • The viewing mode is the one you want: Waiting Room card, "How attendees watch".
  • For our Zoom with Custom stream: "Preview the stream" shows "Stream is arriving" during the rehearsal.
  • Panelists imported ("Import from Speakers") after the speakers are final.
  • "Auto-recording" is "Cloud (recommended)" if you want Zoom's recording (Settings tab, set before the Zoom webinar is created; see A7).
  • The end-of-webinar survey is chosen and the survey itself is open.
  • No other Zoom webinar overlaps (one Zoom licence runs one webinar at a time).
  • Tell the developers the date and time, so nobody deploys or restarts the server during it.

4. Setting up the webinar

What you seeWhyWhat to do
A1Banner "Zoom webinar not provisioned" with "Configure Zoom" and "Run provisioner". Panelists and Recording say "No Zoom webinar attached yet." Zoom is not connected for the organisation, or Zoom refused to create the webinar. An admin checks organisation Settings, Integrations tab, "Zoom Integration": fill in the details, "Save Credentials", "Test Connection". ("Configure Zoom" opens Settings on its first tab: click Integrations.) Then press "Run provisioner".
The toast tells you what happened: "Webinar provisioned" is done; "Anchor session created. Zoom isn't configured" means the details are missing; "Anchor session created, but Zoom webinar creation failed" means Zoom refused.
Developer if it keeps failing while "Test Connection" passes.
A2"Not a Webinar Event"The event's type is not Webinar.Change the type in the event's Settings, then press "Run provisioner". Nothing runs it for you after a type change.
A3The "Video source" choice is missing when creating a webinar, or the Clone dialog shows the partner option greyed out ("not available on this server") The server is not yet set up for partner streams. Nothing to do yourself. Without it, a webinar can only use our Zoom. The choice cannot be changed after the webinar is created: if the wrong one was picked, create the webinar again. Developer to set up partner streams.
A4Saving "How attendees watch" fails: "Custom stream needs the event's Zoom webinar. Run the provisioner first, or keep the Zoom embed." or "Could not set up the custom stream in Zoom: ..." No Zoom webinar yet, or live streaming is switched off in our Zoom account. Run the provisioner first. For the second message, a Zoom account admin switches on "Allow livestreaming of webinars" and "Custom Live Streaming Service" in Zoom, then save again, or press "Re-send stream settings to Zoom".
A5"Too many requests" when saving console settings All the console's settings (lobby, branding, survey, exit button, recording switch, replay link, polls, upvotes) share one limit of 20 saves an hour per webinar. Wait, then save once. Avoid switching things on and off repeatedly on the day.
A6The Zoom embed works for you but attendees on the live site get "Couldn't load the embedded meeting" Zoom's embedding is still in test mode, or our site is not on Zoom's list of allowed sites. An admin sets Integrations, Zoom Integration, "Active SDK Mode" to "Production" and saves. Meanwhile attendees can use "Open in Zoom app instead". Developer or Zoom admin for the allowed sites, or for "Signature is invalid".
A7A switch on the console's Settings tab seems to do nothing Some of those switches are only used when the Zoom webinar is first created, and some are not used at all today. "Auto-recording" counts only when the Zoom webinar is created: to change it afterwards, change it in Zoom. "Automation enabled", "Waiting room" and "Default passcode" do not change anything today: turning Automation off does NOT stop the emails. To stop an email, cancel it in Communications, "Scheduled Emails", "Cancel scheduled email".
A8A cloned webinar has no Zoom webinar, or going live says "Can't open the room: viewing mode is Custom stream but the stream isn't set up in Zoom yet" A clone starts as a Draft with no Zoom webinar, and keeps the viewing mode of the original. On the copy: "Run provisioner", then "Re-send stream settings to Zoom". Also set again what a clone does not copy: the end survey, the exit link, handouts, the replay link, "Show the recording to attendees", live polls, upvotes and chat settings. If the agenda was copied, check the talks are on the webinar's own session (see A9).
A9"Import from Speakers" says "No speakers assigned to the webinar session yet." / "All speakers are already panelists." / "All speakers are missing an email address." Only speakers on the webinar's own session (or its topics) are imported, and only once: someone added or removed later is not changed in Zoom. Put the speakers on the webinar session in the Agenda, add their emails on the Speakers page, and press "Import from Speakers" again. Or add someone by hand with Name, Email and "Add". "Resend the panelist invitation email" sends their link again. Never send "Zoom details (hosts only)" to attendees.
A10After moving the webinar's time: "Session saved, but updating the linked Zoom meeting's time failed" or "... rescheduling the webinar's reminder emails failed" Zoom or the email schedule did not take the new time. Save again from the Agenda. For the emails, press "Re-enqueue" in the console's Email Sequence card. A partner key with its own "Works until" date does not move with the webinar (see D5).

5. Registering and getting in

B1"Registration is closed", a "Closed" button, or "Registration Closed" with "Registration is not currently open for ..." Every new event starts with its registration rates switched off. Or "Registration Open" is off, or no rate is ticked for the main Register link. Registration Types page: tick the box at the left of the rate's row (or "Edit tier", "Active"), and set the price. Check "Main registration link": with nothing ticked the general link always shows "Registration Closed". Event Settings, Registration tab: "Registration Open", then "Save registration settings".
B2"{Rate} registration is currently closed", or on submitting "Pricing tier not found or inactive"That rate or its registration type is switched off.As B1.
B3On submitting: "Registration sales have not started" / "Registration sales have ended"The rate's sales dates.There is no field for these dates on the Registration Types page. Developer
B4"Fully booked", "Registration Full", "Sold out"The event's "Maximum Attendees" or a type's or rate's "Seat Limit" is reached.Raise the limit (event Settings, Registration tab; or the Registration Types page) and save.
B5"Event not found" / "Please check the link and try again."The event is Draft or Cancelled.Event Settings, General, "Status": Published.
B6"Your registration is not active yet" on the webinar page, with "Your registration is awaiting approval..." The type, the rate, or the event requires approval, and nobody has approved it. Registrations, open the person, "Registration Status": Confirmed. Note: no email is sent when you approve, and the person may already have a "You're confirmed" email from when they registered. Tell them they can now join.
B7"Your registration is not active yet" with "You are on the waiting list..." or "Your registration's payment is not complete..." Waiting list, or unpaid. "Registration Status": Confirmed, and "Payment Status": Paid, Complimentary or Inclusive. For many at once: Registrations, tick them, "Payment, badge or cancel".
B8"You're already registered for this event."They registered earlier and lost the email. Copy the attendee page link from the console's status bar ("Copy the attendee page link") and email it. Do not use the registration sheet's "Send Email", "Registration Confirmation": that is the conference confirmation, not the webinar's join email.
B9"Register to join" although they did register They signed in with a different email from the one they registered with, or registered without signing in and have not confirmed their address yet. (A free webinar on an open type lets them in anyway; a paid one or an approval type does not.) Tell them to press "Send confirmation link" in the "Already registered?" box above the button and open the email: the page lets them in by itself once confirmed. Wrong email: sign out and in with the registered address, or change the registration's email (registration sheet, Email, "Change").
B10When registering: "You already have an account with this email. Sign in first..."They have an account already.Tell them to sign in with it ("Sign in instead", or "Forgotten it? Reset"). The password they typed does not change their account.
B11Sign-in fails: "Invalid email or password", "Too many failed sign-in attempts...", "Confirm your email first..."Wrong password, too many tries, or an account never confirmed."Forgot password?" on the event's sign-in page. A password reset does not confirm the address: they still need the confirmation link (B9).
B12Confirmation link errors: "This verification link is invalid or has already been used.", "This verification link has expired.", "You are signed in to a different account..."Old link, or the wrong account open.Ask them to request a new link from the webinar page. Rate limits: "Please wait {n} minute(s) before asking for another link."
B13Times look wrong to the attendeeThe page shows the event's time zone, with its label.Check event Settings, General, "Timezone". Move the webinar's time on the Agenda, which also moves the reminder emails.
B14"Sign in to join" / "This webinar is open to registered attendees only."Not signed in.Sign in; they come back to the webinar page.
B15"Session Not Available" with "Back to Agenda" Most often: the event was set to Completed. The webinar page then stops working for attendees. Or the event is Draft, or Cancelled. Keep "Status" on Published until the replay and survey period is over. After Completed, the replay is only under the attendee's My events. Set it back to Published if needed.
B16"Session hasn't started yet" / "Join opens at {time}"More than 15 minutes before the start.Nothing: this is how it works.
B17On a hospital or office network, the page shows nothing at the top, or stays on "Waiting for stream to start..." while others watch Many people behind one network address can hit our per-address limits. Ask them to try mobile data. Developer if a whole site is affected.
B18"You have been removed from this webinar"The organisers removed this person from the webinar (F10). The page says so instead of playing.If it was a mistake: Webinar Console, Live now card (or its own card after the room closes), Let back in, then ask them to reload.

6. Going live with our Zoom

Everything here is in the Webinar Console. The Setup tab holds the Go live card, the Stream timeline and the Waiting Room card, which has the "Custom stream" box ("Start stream", "Stop stream", "Re-send stream settings to Zoom") and "Preview the stream".

What you seeWhyWhat to do
C1Attendees stuck in the waiting room. The console shows "The scheduled start has passed and the room is still closed" and the badge "Room closed". The room was not opened. "Start as Host" alone does not admit anyone. Go live card: "Open the room / Go live". Or "Start as Host" and choose "Start and open the room" (not "Start only"). Attendees are let in within about 20 seconds, without refreshing.
C2"Can't open the room: viewing mode is Custom stream but the stream isn't set up in Zoom yet..."Streaming was never set up for this Zoom webinar."Re-send stream settings to Zoom" ("Stream settings sent to Zoom"), then open the room.
C3"Zoom refused to start the stream: the webinar is not live in Zoom yet..."The host has not started, or Zoom is in its practice session. Zoom never streams a practice session.The host clicks "Start Webinar" in Zoom, then press "Start stream".
C4"Zoom is still handling the previous start request (it accepts one every 30 seconds)..."Pressed twice quickly.Wait half a minute, check the attendee page, then press once more if needed.
C5"Zoom says this webinar no longer exists. Re-run the provisioner to create it again."The Zoom webinar was deleted in Zoom."Re-run provisioner", then "Re-send stream settings to Zoom" (the new one needs it), then send panelists their new links.
C6"The stream still has not started. Check that the webinar is running in Zoom, then press Start stream..."After opening the room the console tries to start the stream five times, 30 seconds apart, then gives up.Check Zoom is live (not practice), press "Start stream". Last resort, in the Zoom app: More, "Live on Custom Live Streaming Service".
C7"Preview the stream" stays on "No stream yet" with Zoom liveZoom is not sending to us, or is sending with an old password.Press "Re-send stream settings to Zoom", then "Start stream", and keep the preview open (it checks every 10 seconds). Developer if it stays "No stream yet" after that.
C8The Stream timeline shows "Stream dropped", then "Restart requested automatically" (Attempt n of 5). Attendees see "The stream was interrupted" / "Reconnecting automatically". Zoom's push stopped. The system restarts it by itself: it checks every minute and tries up to five times. Usually nothing: it comes back in about a minute and attendees reconnect without refreshing. Press "Start stream" yourself if the timeline says "Automatic restart refused" or after five attempts. Developer if it drops again and again (the reason is only in the server's log, which is cleared after a while: call soon).
C9The stream comes back by itself after you stopped it in ZoomStopping inside Zoom looks like a drop, so it is restarted.Always stop from the console: "Stop stream". The automatic restart then stays off until you press "Start stream" or open the room again.
C10The host ended Zoom by mistake and started again, but attendees have no pictureThe room stays open (a Zoom end counts only after five minutes), but after Zoom said "not running" the automatic restart stops trying.After the host restarts Zoom, press "Start stream".
C11Attendees say the picture is 10 to 20 seconds behind, or their question arrived lateNormal for the Custom stream: about 12 seconds plus Zoom's own delay. The Zoom embed is about 1 second.If near real time matters, use "Zoom embed". To measure: "Our delay" under the preview, and "Measure Zoom delay" with the clock page shared in Zoom.
C12iPhones freeze while others playUneven video pieces, which iPhones handle badly.Attendees reload the page. Developer if many report it: note the time.
C13The second host cannot start their Zoom webinar (Zoom's own message)Our Zoom licence runs one webinar at a time. Zoom still lets you schedule two at once.Do not overlap Zoom webinars. A partner-stream webinar can run alongside one.
C14Stream refused after a short outage or a server updateWhile the site is down, the stream server cannot check the stream's password, so a reconnect is refused.Press "Start stream" once the site is back. Ask developers not to deploy during a webinar.

7. Going live with a partner's stream

A partner's webinar has a "Partner stream" card at the top of the Setup tab: issue the key, "Show the key", "Copy handover message", "Replace the key", "Cut the feed", and a "Signal" line. There is no Zoom webinar and no Panelists tab, and we cannot restart the partner's stream for them.

What you seeWhyWhat to do
D1"This server is not set up to issue a partner's stream key yet..."Server setup missing.Developer
D2Red warning: "The stream server's control connection is not answering." (or "refused", or "gave an answer we could not read")We cannot end a running stream until this is fixed: "Cut the feed" and "Replace" still work for the next connection only.Developer before the webinar. The warning clears by itself once fixed.
D3"Showing the server and stream key needs "See Zoom host details"...", or "Copy handover message" is greyed outYour role cannot see host details; or the key is switched off ("switch it on before you send it") or past its end date ("set a later "Works until" first").Ask someone with that permission to hand it over. "Switch the key on", or set a later "Works until" and "Save details". The key includes its password after the question mark: the partner pastes the whole value.
D4"Signal: Not receiving", or the partner says their platform is refused Wrong server address; the key pasted without the part after the question mark; the key switched off or past its end; or an old key after "Replace". Check with the partner: the whole key, the right server address. "Switch the key on". Set a later "Works until". After a Replace, send the new one ("Copy handover message"). "Not known" means our stream server did not answer the check. Developer if all of that is right.
D5The partner's stream is cut, and admins get "Partner stream cut: the key reached its end date"A key works until 24 hours after the scheduled end (or its own "Works until" date), then the running stream is ended.Set a later "Works until", "Save details". A key with its own date does not move when the webinar is moved.
D6"Can't open the room: the partner's stream key has not been issued..."No key yet.Issue it in the Partner stream card and hand it over first.
D7After opening the room: "The partner's video is not arriving yet..." or "The partner's key is switched off..."The partner has not started, or the key is off.Call the partner; switch the key on. Attendees see "Waiting for stream to start" until it arrives. Check with "Preview the stream".
D8"... but the running stream could not be cut yet: ... It is tried again within a minute."The stream server did not answer when you pressed Replace or Cut.Wait a minute. If the Signal still says "Receiving" after two minutes, press "Cut the feed" again. Developer if it never cuts.
D9Admins get "Partner stream stopped" ("We cannot restart it from here: call the partner")The partner's stream dropped. Not sent after your own Cut, or within three minutes of your own Replace.Phone the partner. "Partner stream is back" follows when it returns.
D10iPhones freeze or viewers buffer on a partner's streamThe partner's encoder settings.Ask for what the card lists under "What to ask the partner's encoder for": H.264 video, AAC audio, a keyframe every 2 seconds (in OBS: Output, "Keyframe Interval" 2), 720p at 30 frames, about 2.5 Mbps.
D11"Replace the key" or "Cut the feed" by mistakeReplace stops the old key and cuts the stream; Cut switches the key off and cuts the stream. The room stays open.After Replace: show the new key and send it. After Cut: "Switch the key on" ("the partner can connect again"). To end the webinar itself, close the room.

9. During the webinar

What you seeWhyWhat to do
F1"Running past the scheduled end..."Information: attendees stay in the live view; the thank-you email and the survey wait until the room closes.Nothing. With the Zoom embed the room closes by itself at the latest 2 hours after the scheduled end: for a longer overrun, re-open it. With a stream it stays open while video arrives, up to 6 hours.
F2The room closed by itself: "Room closed automatically" on the timelineZoom reported the webinar ended, or the safety net (long after the scheduled end).If wrong: "Re-open the room". People with the page still open come back.
F3Finished early, but attendees still see "The stream was interrupted"A Zoom end more than 15 minutes before the scheduled end does not close the room."Close the room", "End the webinar for attendees?", "Close the room". (Closing before the scheduled start counts as a rehearsal.)
F4Attendees cannot see each other's questionsQuestions reach other attendees only once you show them.Q&A tab: "Show to attendees". Upvotes: "Let attendees upvote shown questions". In Zoom embed mode, Q&A is in Zoom itself. Attendees asking a lot see "You have asked a lot of questions this hour" (20 an hour).
F5"Launch" for a poll is greyed out ("Turn on Enable live polls first")Polls are off.Polls tab: "Enable live polls". One poll at a time; turning polls off closes the open one. Results show only to people who answered, with "Show results to attendees who answered".
F6Chat problems ("Chat opens when the session starts.", "The moderator has paused chat.", "You are sending messages too quickly.")Chat settings.Chat tab: "Chat on" (needs a moderator: "Make me the moderator"), "Open in the waiting room", "Mute everyone", "Slow mode" (one message every 15 seconds). "Download the chat (CSV)" afterwards.
F7A handout will not uploadPDF, PPTX or DOCX only, 8 MB each, 10 at most.Shrink or convert the file. "The handout list changed. Refresh and try again." means someone else changed it: refresh.
F8"Live now" shows fewer people than expected, or "No attendees on the page right now."It counts only people with the page open and in view in the last minute, never staff, and only while the room is live. It is not Zoom's count.Nothing: the full attendance is in the reports afterwards.
F9No Q&A, polls or chat on the attendee pageThese are on our page only with the Custom stream (or a partner's stream). In Zoom embed they are inside Zoom.Choose the mode before the day.
F10Someone is misbehaving, or should not be watchingOn the Custom stream the viewers are on our page, not in Zoom, so Zoom cannot remove them.Webinar Console, Live now card: Remove beside their name, then confirm. Within about 30 seconds their page stops the video and says "You have been removed from this webinar"; they can no longer watch, ask, chat, vote or open the replay. Their registration is not changed. Let back in under "Removed from this webinar" undoes it (on the Live now card, or its own card once the room is closed); they see it when they reload. It covers every registration they hold on the event. What it does not do: someone who copied the raw video address can still play it (the page is what stops, not the stream); they still get the webinar emails, and a Zoom recording link in one still works; their earlier questions and chat messages stay, so delete them from the Q&A and Chat tabs. With the Zoom embed, also remove them in Zoom (Participants, Remove), since they could rejoin in the Zoom app.

10. After the webinar

What you or attendees seeWhyWhat to do
G1The survey popup does not appear No survey chosen; the survey is closed or has no questions; the certificate (CME) survey was chosen, which never pops up; the viewer is staff (staff never see it); the webinar is not over; or the event is Completed (B15). Setup tab, "End-of-webinar survey" card: choose a survey and save. Open the survey itself on the Surveys page ("Open for answers"). The card warns "The chosen survey is closed, so nothing pops up..." To email it too: "Also send the link in the thank-you email".
G2An attendee has no certificate ("No certificate yet") Not assigned to it; has not answered the certificate survey (sent by personal link only); that survey is closed; or the attendee never confirmed their account's address. Certificates do not check attendance by themselves. Certificates, "Recipients": assign, or "Add from a list of emails" using the attendance export. Send the survey: registration sheet, "Send Email", "Survey Invitation". Unconfirmed: "Send confirmation link" (B9).
G3No replay for attendees "Show the recording to attendees" is off (it starts off), or Zoom's recording is not in yet. Webinar Console, Recording tab: switch on "Show the recording to attendees". If it says "Attendees cannot see this replay: switch on...", that is the switch.
G4Recording card: "Recording processing", "Fetch failed", "Fetch window expired", or "Cloud recording not yet polled" Zoom's recording is fetched from 10 minutes after the end. If Zoom has none 6 hours after the end, it shows "Fetch window expired" (the "more than 7 days" wording is misleading then): usually cloud recording was off. Check the recording exists in Zoom, then "Refetch now". For a partner's webinar there is no Zoom recording: ask the partner for the file (our own recording of partner streams is being built).
G5The Zoom replay link stops working weeks laterZoom deletes recordings after the account's retention period, and the card still says available.Download from Zoom, upload to Vimeo, paste it in "Replay link (Vimeo)", save, "Preview the saved replay". Vimeo replays do not expire.
G6The Vimeo replay shows a Vimeo error, or anyone with the link can watch it on VimeoThe video's privacy or allowed-sites settings in Vimeo. A showcase or review link is refused ("Must be a Vimeo video link...").Fix in Vimeo, then check with "Preview the saved replay": what you see there is what attendees get.
G7The thank-you email had no replay, or the old oneIt is sent once, about 5 minutes after the room closes, and mentions a replay only if the switch was on by then. It is never re-sent.Switch the recording on before the end. Afterwards, send the replay link in an email of your own.
G8Attendance: "Attendance will appear here after the session ends and Zoom finalizes..." or "Attendance report not ready yet"Zoom's report comes about 30 minutes after the end, and is fetched for 30 days.Wait, or "Sync now" (after the end). With the Custom stream or a partner's stream, the audience is in "Attendance on our page" (Zoom's report lists only the speakers then). Export within 90 days: the minute-by-minute record is archived after that.
G9Post-event report: "The webinar has not ended yet: these figures are so far." or attendance 0Not closed yet, or Zoom's report not synced yet.Close the room, wait for the attendance sync. Tick "Include survey comments in the PDF" only after reading them. "Too many requests" means 30 PDFs an hour was reached.
G10Email Sequence row shows "x/y sent (n failed)", or "Webinar email requested but event has no anchor session...", or "Stuck in processing..."Some addresses bounced, the webinar was never provisioned, or a send was interrupted.Communications, "Scheduled Emails", "Retry scheduled email"; "Failed recipients" shows who. Without a session: "Run provisioner", then "Re-enqueue" (it never re-sends a step already sent).

11. Things the system does not do (yet)

Found while writing this guide. Each is a gap, not something you are doing wrong. Plan around them until they are fixed.

  • No email is sent when you approve a registration (B6), and someone pending approval may already have a "You're confirmed" email.
  • "Automation enabled", "Waiting room" and "Default passcode" on the console's Settings tab do nothing; "Auto-recording" counts only when the Zoom webinar is created (A7).
  • A rate's sales dates cannot be set on the Registration Types page (B3).
  • Setting the event to Completed stops the webinar page, its survey and its replay for attendees (B15).
  • A pending, waiting-list or unpaid registration made while signed out shows "Register to join" instead of the real reason (B9).
  • With the Zoom embed and no embedding details for the organisation, attendees see "In meeting" with nothing playing (E13).
  • Panelists are not kept in step with the speakers: press "Import from Speakers" after every change (A9).
  • The webinar emails go to every Confirmed registration, speakers and tests included (G11).
  • Only one of our Zoom webinars can run at a time (C13).
  • We do not yet record a partner's stream ourselves (G4).
  • "Fetch window expired" can appear 6 hours after the end, while the card's text speaks of 7 days (G4).
  • Removing a viewer stops their page, not the video stream itself: a copied stream address keeps playing, and the webinar emails still reach them (F10).

12. When you call a developer

Send, in one message:

  • The webinar's name, and the link to its Webinar Console.
  • The time it happened (with the time zone), and whether it is still happening.
  • The exact words on screen, or a screenshot (ideally of the console and of the attendee page).
  • For an attendee problem: their email address, their device and browser (iPhone Safari, Chrome on Windows...), and their network if it is a hospital or office.
  • What you already tried from this guide.

Call quickly for a stream that drops again and again: the server's own record of why is cleared after a while.
