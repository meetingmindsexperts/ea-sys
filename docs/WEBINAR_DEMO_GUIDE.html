Webinar practice guide

Run one practice webinar end to end, with the team playing producer, speakers and attendees. The main thing to prove: attendees watch inside our event page and are never sent off to the Zoom app.

This runs on the live system, with a real Zoom webinar

  • Creating a webinar event makes a real webinar in the company Zoom account and schedules its reminder emails.
  • Only team members register, with their own work email. Nobody outside the team gets an email.
  • Keep the event name obviously a practice, e.g. Webinar Practice, do not share, and never post its link.
  • This will be the first time attendees join through our page on the live system. Past webinars sent people to Zoom directly. So watch closely and write down anything odd, with the browser and device.

1. What attendees see

Every webinar email links to our event page, not to Zoom. From there:

  1. Register on the event's page (name, email, a password). That creates their account.
  2. Emails arrive: a reminder the day before, one an hour before, a "we are live" note and a thank-you with the recording. Each has a button back to the session page.
  3. Sign in on the session page. Unregistered visitors only see a sign-in or register prompt.
  4. Waiting room. Before the producer opens the room they see the event banner (or a holding video), a countdown and "You're in the waiting room. Keep this page open. You'll join automatically, right here, when the host opens the session." There is no Join button yet, on purpose.
  5. Admitted automatically. Within about 20 seconds of the producer opening the room, the page switches to "Live now" and the webinar plays inside the Live Video tab of our page. The card above it reads "Join the webinar without leaving this page."
  6. After the event, the same page offers Watch Replay once the recording has come in.

What attendees should never see

  • A browser prompt "Open zoom.us?" or "Open Zoom Meetings?"
  • A Zoom download page, or the Zoom app starting.

Allowing the browser to use the speaker or microphone is normal: that is the browser, not the Zoom app. If the in-page player fails, the page shows the error and a button Open in Zoom app instead. It never switches to Zoom on its own. If anyone sees that button during practice, note their browser and device.

Speakers and the host are different: they run the webinar from Zoom itself, with their own links (next section).

3. Setup, the day before

By the producer, about 20 minutes.

  1. Create the event. Events › New event, choose Webinar as the event type, name it Webinar Practice, do not share, set tomorrow's date and a one-hour slot. Saving creates the anchor session, the Zoom webinar and the email sequence by itself.
  2. Check the console. Open the event's Webinar Console. The Overview card shows the Zoom webinar's join URL and passcode, and the Email Sequence card lists the four emails. If something is missing, click Re-run provisioner.
  3. Dress the waiting room (Setup tab, Waiting Room card):
    • How attendees watch: keep Zoom embed, interactive, native Q&A/chat.
    • Optional: a YouTube or Vimeo Holding video, a Waiting room image, and a Lobby message.
    • Click Save lobby settings.
  4. Add the speakers. Add them as speakers on the event, then in the Panelists card click Import from Speakers (or add them there directly). Each gets an email with a personal panelist link.
  5. Publish the event: event Settings, status Published. While it is a Draft the room always counts as open, so nobody would see a waiting room.
  6. Attendees register. Send the event's public link to the attendee group only. Each registers with their own work email and a password.

5. Checklist: tick as you go

Attendees (each person, note browser and device)

  • The reminder email's button opens our session page, not Zoom.
  • Before the room opens: waiting room with countdown, and no Join button.
  • After the room opens: admitted within about 20 seconds without clicking anything.
  • The webinar plays inside our page. No "Open Zoom?" prompt, no Zoom download page, no Zoom app.
  • Sound works, and Q&A or chat can be sent from inside the page.
  • Closing and reopening the page brings them straight back in (at most one click on Join now, still inside our page).
  • On a phone, the player fits the screen and is usable.

Producer

  • Every attendee appears in the Live now card, and moves from Lobby to Joined.
  • Closing the room sends attendees back to the waiting room, and reopening admits them again.
  • The next day: attendance, polls and Q&A show in Analytics, and the replay is available.

Speakers

  • The panelist invitation arrived and its link opened Zoom as a panelist.
  • They could share a screen and be seen and heard on the attendees' pages.

6. Clean-up

  1. An admin deletes the practice event.
  2. Deleting the event does not delete the Zoom webinar: remove it at zoom.us, under Webinars.
  3. The team members' practice attendee accounts can stay; they do no harm.
  4. Send the notes from the checklist, with any screenshots, to the EA-SYS team.

7. If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Attendee sees "Sign in to join"They are not signed in. Sign in with the email and password they registered with.
"You must be registered for this event"Signed in with a different email from the one they registered. Register that email, or sign in with the other.
Stuck in the waiting room after the room openedWait 30 seconds; it checks every 15 to 20. Still stuck: refresh the page. Note it on the checklist.
The player shows an error and "Open in Zoom app instead"Note the browser, device and the error text, then use the button so they can keep watching. This is exactly what the practice is looking for.
Someone missing from the Live now cardIt counts pages open in the last 60 seconds. Ask them to keep the tab in front for a minute.
No reminder emailsCheck the Email Sequence card; Re-enqueue recreates it. A reminder whose time has already passed is skipped.
